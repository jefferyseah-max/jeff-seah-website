How it arrives

In your Google Calendar, not in another app

Delivered into your own calendar

Each month's entries are written directly into your personal Google Calendar. Open the day, read the note, plan around it. Nothing to download, nothing to log in to.

Works with the Google Calendar you already use

Read from your chart, every month

The month's energy meets your four pillars differently from anyone else's. I read that meeting and mark the days that matter for you: favorable days for the things you want to start or sign, caution days for the things you should not.

Hours, not just days

On the days that matter, the entry names the best hours and the usable ones, so a meeting or a signing can sit in the right window. The rest of the day is yours.
