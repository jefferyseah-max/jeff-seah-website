Privacy

What I collect, and what I do with it

Last updated 17 November 2026

I ask for your birth details because a reading cannot be prepared without them. They are used for your readings and nothing else.

Who is responsible

This site is run by Jeff Seah, an individual based in Singapore, trading as jeffseah.rocks. I am the person responsible for your personal data, and I handle it in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).

For any question about your data, email Jeff.

What I collect

When you pay

Checkout runs on a secure payment provider, which collects your name, email address and payment details. Your card details are handled by the payment provider and I never see them. I see your name, your email, what you bought and whether the payment went through.

When you send your details for a reading

After payment, a short form asks for what the reading is calculated from and written around:

  • Your full name and the email address for delivery.
  • Date, time and place of birth, and whether the birth time is unknown.
  • Gender, which is a calculation input for the chart.
  • For the 2027 Annual Outlook: your current work status, your reading focus, any circumstances you choose to share, your questions or decisions for the year (up to five), any topics you want left out, and the time zone your report is written for.
  • For monthly plans: the calendar account your calendar should be shared with, if it differs from your email.
  • Whether the reading is a gift. If you enter someone else's details, you confirm that you have their consent.

When you book a session

Single sessions are booked and paid through an online booking service, which collects your name, email and chosen time. If your questions need a reading, I ask for your birth details by email before the session.

When you visit

I use measurement tools to count page visits and to see, in total, how visitors find the site, use its tools, watch its videos and respond to offers. This includes active time on the page, scroll progress and checkout clicks. Anonymous engagement records are kept for up to 90 days. Some measurement tools may store a small amount of information in your browser, such as a cookie or a campaign tag kept for up to 30 days. They do not receive your birth details, your questions or your report.

How I use it

  • To calculate your chart and prepare, review and deliver your readings.
  • To send your order emails and the follow-up emails described below.
  • To answer you when you write to me.
  • To see, in total, which pages and campaigns lead to orders.

I do not sell personal data, and I do not use your birth details or your questions for marketing.

Where it is kept

Intake forms are stored in secure cloud storage and email accounts, and used only to prepare that person's readings. Reports are hosted privately: each one sits behind a private link on my report hosting or in the client portal, and is not listed or indexed anywhere public.

Monthly subscribers receive their calendars as a shared online calendar, linked to the account they name.

Email

Your name, email address and order status go to my email service so that order emails and follow-ups reach you. Birth details, questions and exclusions are never sent there. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe, and unsubscribing does not affect a reading you have paid for.

Who helps me process it

I work with a small number of service providers for payments, email, cloud storage, hosting and the software tools I use to prepare and deliver readings. They process data on my behalf, for those purposes only. Some of them store data outside Singapore. Beyond these providers, I share your data with nobody unless the law requires it.

How long I keep it

I keep your details for as long as you are a client, so that future readings can build on earlier ones without asking you again. Ask me to delete them and I will, apart from the payment records I am required to keep by law.

Your choices

  • See it. Ask what I hold about you and I will send it.
  • Correct it. Tell me what is wrong and I will fix it. A corrected birth time can change a reading, so it is worth doing.
  • Delete it. Ask, and I will remove your details and your reports.
  • Stop marketing email. Use the unsubscribe link in any email, or write to me.

To do any of these, email Jeff. I reply within 30 days, and usually much sooner. If you are not satisfied with my answer, you may contact Singapore's Personal Data Protection Commission.

Changes to this page

If what I collect or how I use it changes, I will update this page and the date at the top. The rules for orders and refunds are in the terms.
