YOUR FIRST SIGN-IN

A few simple steps.
No password to remember.

Use the email address registered for your coaching reports. Your sign-in code will arrive in that inbox.

  1. 01

    Open your portal

    Click Open your portal, then choose Sign in with email on the welcome screen.

    The portal opens in a new tab, so you can keep this guide beside you.

  2. 02

    Enter your registered email

    Type the email address you use for your coaching reports, then request your login code.

    Use the same address even if you have more than one email account.

  3. 03

    Check your inbox

    Look for an email from Cloudflare Access with a six-digit code. Cloudflare provides the secure sign-in for your portal.

    Your code is valid for 10 minutes. If you request another, use the newest code.

  4. 04

    Return and enter your code

    Go back to the portal sign-in tab, enter your six-digit code and finish signing in. You’ll arrive in your own personal portal.

    On your own device, you can choose to remember the browser after verifying your email.

Need a hand signing in?

My code hasn’t arrived

First, check that you entered the email address registered for your reports. Check that inbox’s spam or junk folder too.

If it still hasn’t arrived, reply to the email where I shared this guide so I can check your sign-in address.

My code has expired or doesn’t work

Request a fresh code from the sign-in screen and use only the newest email. Requesting another code replaces the previous one.

Do I need a password?

No. Use your registered email and the code sent to that inbox. If you sign out, you’ll need to verify your email again.

How do I return next time?

Bookmark your portal at reports.jeffseah.rocks. You can also keep this guide to revisit the walkthrough.
